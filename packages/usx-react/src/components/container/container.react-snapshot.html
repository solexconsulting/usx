Editorial Feature

From our neighborhoods

A new chapter for the Riverside library

A familiar neighborhood space is opening its doors to more ways to learn, connect, and get things done.
Cedar County library teamSeptember 14, 2026 · 4 minute read

Room for the whole neighborhood

On a typical afternoon, the library hosts students working on a science project, neighbors trading gardening tips, and job seekers getting help with applications. The renewed Riverside branch makes room for all of them.

More ways to use your library

  • Two reservable meeting rooms
  • A family reading corner
  • Six additional public computers
Residents helped shape the renovation through open houses and small group conversations. Their priorities were practical: comfortable places to sit, accessible workspaces, and room for activities without interrupting quiet study.
The result includes brighter reading areas and a flexible community room. Staff can set up the room for a language class in the morning and a neighborhood meeting in the evening.
A new welcome desk brings library staff and community navigators together. Visitors can ask about a book, get help finding a service, or learn how to reserve a computer in one place.

Built around your day

Weekly activities include family story time, guided technology practice, and conversation groups. Most programs are free and welcome drop-in visitors.
Stop by during opening week to meet the team, explore the new spaces, and share an idea for a future program.

  • Monday–Thursday: 9 a.m.–8 p.m.
  • Friday–Saturday: 9 a.m.–5 p.m.

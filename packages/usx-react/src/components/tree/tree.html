    Application Help

    Help with your application

    Find answers to common questions about submitting your application.

    • Uploading supporting documents
      • Which file formats can I upload?

        You can upload PDF, JPG, or PNG files. Each file must be smaller than 10 MB.

      • What if I do not have a scanner?

        You can use a clear photo of the document. Include all four corners and make sure the text is readable.

      • Read the supporting document checklist
    • Where do I find my application number?

      Your application number is in the confirmation email sent after you submitted your application. Check your spam folder if you cannot find the email.

    • Can someone help me complete the form?

      A service center can help you complete your application. Language assistance is available on request.

    • Contact the application support team

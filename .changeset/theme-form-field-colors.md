Add shared background, text, and placeholder theme tokens for text-entry fields, selects, and the visible inputs and option lists of composite form controls. Their defaults follow the surface and text roles; prebuilt and random dark themes use the deeper surface for field backgrounds. The Theme Playground groups them under Input. ComboBox and TimePicker icons follow the input text color. The exact USWDS Select icon contrasts with its field surface through CSS.
